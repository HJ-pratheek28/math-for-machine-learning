# Mastery Roadmap — Follow the Book, Not a Countdown

This replaces the old 12-week sprint. There is no fixed completion deadline: move forward when you pass the mastery gate, not when a calendar says so.

## The single primary resource

Follow **[Mathematics for Machine Learning](https://mml-book.github.io/)** by Deisenroth, Faisal, and Ong in chapter order. The book is the spine; this roadmap only tells you how to study it deeply.

Use [MASTER_CURRICULUM.md](MASTER_CURRICULUM.md) for chapter-level goals, derivations, and labs. Use [MASTERY_PROTOCOL.md](MASTERY_PROTOCOL.md) to decide whether a topic is genuinely complete.

## Stages

1. **Readiness:** repair only the algebra, functions, logarithms, summation, or notation gaps that stop you understanding the text.
2. **Chapter 2 — Linear algebra:** maps, systems, vector spaces, rank, and dimensions.
3. **Chapter 3 — Analytic geometry:** inner products, norms, distances, orthogonality, and projections.
4. **Chapter 4 — Matrix decompositions:** eigenvalues, eigenvectors, diagonalization, and SVD.
5. **Chapter 5 — Vector calculus:** derivatives, gradients, Jacobians, and chain rule.
6. **Chapter 6 — Probability:** distributions, expectation, variance, covariance, and conditional probability.
7. **Chapter 7 — Optimization:** objectives, gradients, curvature, and iterative methods.
8. **Chapter 8 — Models meet data:** connect the mathematics to learning problems and assumptions.
9. **Chapter 9 — Linear regression:** derive and implement least squares.
10. **Chapter 10 — PCA:** connect variance, eigendecomposition, and SVD.
11. **Chapter 11 — Gaussian mixture models:** likelihood, latent variables, and EM.
12. **Chapter 12 — Support vector machines:** margins and optimization.
13. **Synthesis:** build the capstone described in the master curriculum.

## A study session

Adapt the length to your schedule; consistency matters more than an arbitrary timer.

- Read a small section with a pencil.
- Close the book and reconstruct the key ideas.
- Work derivations and exercises on paper.
- Implement one concept or verify one result in Python/NumPy.
- Write down the most important mistake and what caused it.

Do not try to code every equation immediately. The order is understanding first, then independent derivation, then implementation and testing.

## Definition of done

A topic is complete only when it passes the rubric in [MASTERY_PROTOCOL.md](MASTERY_PROTOCOL.md): conceptual understanding, derivation, hand problems, implementation and numerical validation, and transfer to unfamiliar problems.

## After this book

Only after completing the core spine should you pick a specialization: deep learning mathematics, probabilistic ML, optimization/statistical learning theory, or research-oriented proof skills. The book builds a foundation; it does not replace advanced specialization.
