# Curriculum Roadmap

The roadmap is a planning guide, not a deadline. Do not skip a mastery gate to stay on schedule.

| Stage | Focus | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Months 1–2 | Foundations and linear algebra | Least-squares derivation, projections, SVD/PCA experiments |
| Months 3–4 | Calculus and probability | Gradient checks, simulations, estimator derivations |
| Months 5–6 | Statistics and optimization | Estimation experiments and optimizer benchmarks |
| Months 7–8 | Information theory and numerical mathematics | Entropy/KL experiments and stability audit |
| Months 9–10 | Advanced topics | A selected research result reproduced and analyzed |
| Months 11–12 | Integration and research | Reproducible capstone and public release |

[Read the full topic-level roadmap in GitHub](https://github.com/HJ-pratheek28/math-for-machine-learning/blob/main/docs/curriculum/roadmap.md).
